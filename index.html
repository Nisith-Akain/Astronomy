Beyond the planets

Past Neptune the Sun is just a bright star. At 30 AU its light is about 900 times fainter than at Earth.

A thin line home

The high-gain dish is the probe's only link to Earth. From the edge of the Solar System a radio signal takes nearly a day to arrive.

Into interstellar space

Voyager 1, launched in 1977, crossed the heliopause in 2012, the first spacecraft to leave the Sun's bubble of solar wind.
